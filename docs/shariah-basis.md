## 1. What is obligated (Qaza of what?)

1. Qaza is **Fard rakats of the 5 farz namazain** + **Witr (Wajib)**. No qaza of sunnah or nafl.
   - Fajr: 2 fard rakats
   - Zuhr: 4 fard rakats
   - Asr: 4 fard rakats
   - Maghrib: 3 fard rakats
   - Isha: 4 fard rakats **+ 3 witr (wajib)**
2. Witr is counted as part of Isha, not a separate namaz — but it is qaza-obligated.
3. **Total per missed day = 17 fard rakats + 3 witr = 20 rakats.**

**Source:** Video 1 (Maulana Ilyas Qadri, @FjFz1zdBPS4), Video 3 (@L25xPDo7cbw), Video 4 (Allama Tahir Madani, @YyL5HvOXKuM lines 1–10), Video 5 (Allama Azhar Saeed, @KTyzis5k4gc lines 192–208).

## 2. Puberty / starting point of the count

- If the person **remembers** the age/date of bulugh (puberty, or when salah became fard on them), count from there.
- If **not remembered** — ease the count from the standard estimate:
  - **Boy: from 12 years of age** (bulugh typically 12–15, signs begin ~12; precaution = count from 12)
  - **Girl: from 9 years of age**

**Source:** Video 1 lines 11–19; Video 5 lines 264–284.

## 3. Niyyat (intention) for lifetime qaza with unknown count

Three valid methods, all approved by ulama:

1. **"Sab se pehli" method (recommended):** Niyyat each time: *"Ya Allah, the first qaza-honedarfazli Fajr (etc.) that became fard on me after bulugh, which I have not yet prayed — I am praying it."* Every time you pray with this niyyat, that one is discharged and the *next* one becomes "first". Repeat the same sentence every time.
2. **"Aakhri" method (equally valid): Same but from the latest side:** *"…the last Fajr that I left/missed and have not yet prayed."* Working backwards until the two ends meet.
3. **Per-prayer method:** If the person remembers exactly (or the app tracks it): niyyat of the specific prayer ("the Zuhr of 2nd of such-and-such month").
4. Niyyat of the heart (puqhta irada) is the essence; spoken words are **mustahab, not required** (not one of fard/wajib).
5. The niyyat should be at hand entering salaam's time — during takbir (mukhtassar rakaat of niyyat near Allaahu Akbar).

**Source:** Video 2 (@4ogS2LTQCXg lines 10–34); Video 4 lines 44–74; Video 5 lines 88–119.

## 4. When can qaza be prayed — Makruh times

Qaza can be prayed **any moment of the 24 hours** except the **three makruh times** (also no ada/namaz there):

| # | Makruh period | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sunrise (tulu-e-aftab) | from sun rising until ~15–20 min after (before fajr's karahat ends) |
| 2 | Zawal / Jawal-e-Kubra (sun at its zenith) | ~20–30 min before dhuha begins (country-dependent) — roughly around noon |
| 3 | Sunset (ghurub-e-aftab) | last ~15–20 minutes of Asr until sun fully sets |

Exception: current day's **Asr** can be prayed in the (3) window, though delay is makruh/sin.

Additional decorum:
- After **Asr's 4 fard**, nafl is makruh — so **do not pray qaza in the masjid / in public view** at such times; pray hidden/at home. Publicly praying when people would assume nafl = Sin of publicly reminding of one's qaza.
- Never **announce** one's qaza ( gunah-e-izhaar );
- After Fajr's storia and Maghrib/Isha: allowed — all day/night except the 3 windows.
- In a jamaat/qaza/time (jamaat-e-umoomi), qaza can be prayed in jamaat only behind **Imam-e-Umoomi** ⚠ (standard Hanafi position; confirm before building a "jamaat qaza" feature). Not covered in videos.

**Source:** Video 3 lines 16–34, 62–66; Video 4 lines 17–44 & 35–43; Video 5 lines 125–161.

## 5. Which prayer's waqt can be used for which qaza

**No binding of qaza Fajr ↔ Fajr waqt.** You may pray qaza of any prayer in any of the five waqts (outside makruh times):
- Fajr's qaza can be prayed at Isha time, Zuhr's at Fajr time, etc.
- After Fajr's waqt and Asr's waqt end → not allowed when / where nafl would be kharasti… clearly only the **3 makruh windows** block qaza; everything else is allowed per video 5.
- Common practice offered by Allama Tahir Madani: pray each new qaza at its matching normal time slot (Fajr-qaza in Fajr, Zuhr-qaza in Zuhr …), or pray **all 6 (5 fard + witr covering one day's set) at the time of any one prayer** — e.g., after Isha today, pray tomorrow's-share = one full day (6 prayers). Progress = "one day less" per day.

**Source:** Video 4 lines 74–89; Video 5 lines 162–190.

## 6. Rukhsat-e-Takhfeef (ease rulings) for heavy qaza

Ulama gave 4 shortcut ri'ayat so a person with decades of qaza can finish sooner — **all explicitly valid, not "rushed"/bidi'at**:

**6.1 — Skip before recitation:** skip Sana, Ta'awwudh (a'oodhu billah…), Tasmiyah (bismillah…); start directly from **Surah Fatiha**; recite a **short surah** and go to ruku.

**6.2 — Skip recitation in 3rd/4th rakaat of fard:** instead of Surah Fatiha, the duration of **3× "Subhan Allah"**, then go to ruku. (Reciting Fatiha there is mustahab — a sahabi is reported on record not reciting it.)

**6.3 — Shorten tashahhud's durud and dua:** pray tashahhud; at the point of durud, only *"Allghumma salli 'ala Muhammadin wa 'ala Aali Muhammad"* — **skip full Durood-e-Ibrahimi and skip Dua-e-Masura** — then turn salam.

**6.4 — Tasbihaat reduced to once:** ruku: *"Subhana Rabbiyal-Azeem"* once; rising: *"Rabbana lakal-hamd"*; sujud: *"Subhana Rabbiyal-A'la"* once — that's a valid ruku/sujud.
- The one-time tasbih IS valid per mufti — no restriction means it should not be so rushed that the body is incomplete (arqan still required): ruku still has a complete pause of standing, jalsa sits properly, etc.
- Witr: in place of Dua-e-Qunoot, say **"Allghumma-ghfir li" 1× (or 3×)** — 1× is valid, 3× better; or "Rabbi-ghfir li". ⚠ In video 1 "Alf " (Figuhi) is used for the same — confirm exact wording preference from Bahar-e-Shariat.

**Also explicit:**
- "GeldigGENupid" (read loudly fast) is NOT the shortcut — the takhfeef above is the correct method; wording/articulation (makharij) of hurf must remain intact, do not mumble/destroy harakat.
- Sujood/ruku **shud (stillness) is wajib** — no kaif (pause) shortening on those; some narrations of over-imposed "half-standing" hospitality to jlosa. The 4 shortcuts are the permitted list, not "just rush".

**Source:** Video 3 lines 1–15; Video 4 lines 115–161; Video 5 lines 209–257.

## 7. Special cases & border rulings

7.1 **Deliberately missed (bi-qastr):** salah was fard but the person skipped it knowingly → the qaza is obligatory **and tauba (repentance) is also required** — qaza alone does not erase the sin of deliberate miss. (Videos 2, 5.)

7.2 **Hastening:** delaying qaza without uzr (excuse) is sin. Ulama: begin now, don't judge "I'll start at 70". Pre-planning nonsense like "I'll start at 40" is dangerous; death may arrive first and, after cave, no moment — a person who "planned" and dies is left with regret. (Video 2 lines 44–84.)

7.3 **Number offset (exchange) and amount per day:**
- **Unlimited per day.** No cap (koi ta'dud mahdoora nahi). Pray as many as needed ASAP. (Video 4 lines 74–79; Video 5 lines 162–180.)
- **One must not think of "just 1 qaza per prayer (roll call)"** — that concept ("har namaz ke saath ek kaza") is WRONG — the whole burden must be lifted. (Video 1 lines 27–31.)

7.4 **Fidya:** Cannot substitute rakats with money/feeding etc. No fidya for salah. (Video 3 line 98.)

7.5 **Friday missed:** If Jumma itself was missed, nothing more is needed for Jumma — but if Zuhr (the waqt of Friday) also passed unprayed → its fard/Zuhr qaza is due; if Jumma skipped deliberately → tauba also. (Video 3 lines 66–70.)

7.6 **Eid and Tarawih:** no qaza once their waqt passed. Eid is wajib — skipping deliberately → gunah + tauba. Tarawih skipped → sunnah-level (nafl) deficiency, tauba istighfar of the nafl deficiency is commendable. (Video 3 lines 70–76.)

7.7 **Sunnah of Fajr:** not a qaza-obligation; permissive/mustahab to recoup it during the window **after sunrise (~20 min after) until zawal / nisf-ul-aftab** if it was missed in sleep; per some narrations, if missed deliberately it should not be recouped — ⚠ the videos state it flexible as mustahab in that window only. Also warns that the sunnah other than Fajr's has no qaza at all. (Video 3 lines 76–86.)

7.8 **Safar/Halat-ghaba (travel):** Islam's "qaza follows the halat in which the prayer lapsed":
- Qaza of a **safar (travel)**-missed prayer — if the missed prayer was halat-e-safar (2 rakats for 4-rakat fars), then qaza stays **2 rakats** even if the person is now muqim (in residence) — because that was his halat when it lapsed.
- A muqim (resident) whose Zuhr lapsed at home, then travels — prayers **4 rakats** on the road as qaza because halat-e-ikamat is when it lapsed.
- (This applies only to 4-rakat fard: Zuhr, Asr, Isha. Fajr 2, Maghrib 3, Witr 3 unchanged by travel/halat anyway — but note: **qasa of Witr** can be skippable under safar… ⚠ not in videos; follow written fatawa.)

**Source:** Video 4 lines 96–117.

7.9 **Manzoori (genuinely unburdened)** — not covered in videos. **⚠ VERIFY with mufti** — critical for female users: salah missed during **Haidh (menstruation) and Nifas (bleeding of child-birth)** — in Hanafi/Ahle-Sunnat fiqh these are **COMPLETELY EXEMPT — no qaza, no count**. Not covered by the 5 videos but is a foundational ruling (qurtus… narrate Bahar-e-Shariat). The app MUST ask gender and handle this. Confirm source citation before shipping to female users.

7.10 **Sleep/delusion-forgotten miss (naseeyan):** If missed due to forgetting or sleep — still a qaza of rakats, but **no sin**; tauba not required for this case — only adaa. (Video 5 lines 71–83.)

7.11 **"Jumma-tul-Wida Khatama" superstition:** The viral claim that 4 sunnah rakats of "last Friday of Ramadan" (Jumu'at al-Wida) forgives a lifetime of qaza is **FALSE — no sahih basis; "bakwas"** per Allama Azhar Saeed. Fard's qaza stays on the person until actually prayed. App should warn users against this shortcut myth. (Video 5 lines 291–307.)

7.12 **Qaza in masjid vs home:** in the windows where nafl is makruh (post-Asr, post-Fajr; also famous opinal — "before zawal"), pray qaza **at home**, and certainly **do not pray qaza in the masjid if someone might report you** — this would be izhar-e-gunaah. (Video 2 lines 80–87; Video 4 lines 32–43.)

7.13 **Kifayat against sitting:** namaz-e-qaza must generally be prayed standing like any fard; sitting (bila uzr) is invalid. Qaza with uzr (illness) follows normal fiqh of praying sitting/lying — not covered in detail in videos. ⚠ VERIFY rule variants for ill / elderly users.

### Newly found rulings (beyond the videos) — prōōf sourced:

10.1 **Tarteeb is WAJIB among outstanding qaza namazain (Alamgiri / Durr-e-Mukhtar ma' Radd-ul-Muhtar):**
- While **six (5 fard + witr) are outstanding**, order must be maintained: Fajr → Zuhr → Asr → Maghrib → Isha → Witr. You cannot skip a qaza of Fajr and pray qaza of Isha while 6+ remain.
- **As soon as fewer than 6 remain**, that tarteeb requirement drops (one can pray any of the remaining in any order).
- If some were prayed unaware — e.g., user remembers "Fajr 2, Isha 1 missing…" — the app's ordered queue model naturally satisfies this if it anchors one qaza per specific day-slot.

10.2 **No nafl accepted while a fard qaza is owed** — mustahab ways lie aside; nafil normally should not be prayed while fard-qaza backlog exists (per one fiqh-guide: "nafil not accepted if qaza of fard is owed"). App should discourage/flag nafl-slot logging while backlog > 0, or allow with gentle guidance.

10.3 **"Best estimate" (güman-e-ghālib) counting**: for a person who was negligent, the ulama give the method: estimate bulugh time, aim free-time estimates, count per-year, **taqdeer basis**, and "jitne pe dil jama jaye us se kuch zyada padh jaaye" (count/note with margin, tadha-padh is better than lesser). If years overlap with mixed periods of praying/missing — count only the windows certainly LOST; praying an "extra" margin estimate — better. App supports a margin (safety buffer) toggle.

10.4 **Priority guidance**: farz first then wajib — within backlog, farz rakats complete before witr backlog is zero'd; each day's full set (Fajr→…→Witr) counts toward progress.
